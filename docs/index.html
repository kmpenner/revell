--- layout: default title: Home --- # The Revell Digital Corpus Welcome to the digital edition of the collected works of **Professor E.J. Revell** (1934-2022). This project aims to make the scholarly contributions of E.J. Revell accessible to a wider audience through a digitized, searchable corpus of his essays and articles on Hebrew linguistics, Masoretic studies, and biblical text criticism. ## Biography Ernest John Revell was a distinguished scholar in the field of Near Eastern Studies, specializing in Hebrew linguistics and the Masoretic tradition. He served as a professor at the University of Toronto, where he influenced generations of students and contributed significantly to the understanding of Biblical Hebrew.
