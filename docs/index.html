Prof. Ernest John Revell
(1934–2017)

Ernest John Revell was one of the twentieth century's preeminent scholars of Biblical Hebrew, Masoretic studies, and Semitic linguistics. As professor and Chair of the Department of Near and Middle Eastern Civilizations at the University of Toronto, he transformed our understanding of biblical vocalization, cantillation accents, and prose syntax.

This digital edition makes Revell's research openly accessible through high-resolution archival facsimiles, full-text transcriptions encoded in Text Encoding Initiative (TEI) XML, item-level catalogue records, and an interactive full-text search engine.
